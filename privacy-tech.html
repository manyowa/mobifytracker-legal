MobifyTracker Tech App — Privacy Policy

Android and iOS app for MobifyTracker technicians · Manyowa Nong

This Privacy Policy explains how Manyowa Nong ("MobifyTracker", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information through the MobifyTracker Tech App (the "App"). The App is a work tool for MobifyTracker's own technicians and installers; it is not offered to the public, and you can only sign in with an account we issue to you. We process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa.

Effective 19 November 2026 · Last updated 19 November 2026

1. Who we are

MobifyTracker fits, maintains and supports vehicle tracking units for taxi and fleet owners. Technicians use the App to receive and complete jobs (fitments, repairs, inspections), record what was done, talk to the team over a push-to-talk radio, and share their location with the office while they work.

Responsible party: Manyowa Nong, South Africa
Information Officer / contact: ceo@sharedmobility.africa

2. Information about you (the technician)

  • Account: your name, username or mobile number, technician id and role. Your password is checked by our servers and is not stored in readable form on your phone.
  • Location: your phone's precise location. The App asks for location "all the time" because the office uses it to dispatch the nearest technician, to locate you when needed during your work, to share your live location while a job is in progress, and to record where a job was started and completed. Location is used only for this work.
  • Voice: voice notes you record on a job, and push-to-talk radio messages you send to the team. Radio messages are delivered to your team through our voice provider (LiveKit); a message recorded while you have no signal is uploaded when you reconnect.
  • Photos and scans: photos you take or choose from your gallery for a job, your signature and the customer's signature, and barcodes or text you scan (tracking-unit serial numbers, vehicle VIN and engine numbers).
  • Device: a push-notification token so we can send you jobs and alerts, and basic technical information (app version, device model, operating system).
  • Usage and diagnostics: we use PostHog (product analytics) and Sentry (crash reporting). PostHog records which screens you open and which controls you tap, linked to your technician id, and makes a screen recording of each App session (screenshots of the App's own screens and your taps) so we can see where the App is hard to use. Text you type and screens showing customer personal details are hidden in these recordings, and recordings are deleted after 30 days. Sentry records crash and error diagnostics and performance timings, linked only to your technician id.

3. Information about our customers

To complete a job the App shows, and lets you record, information about the vehicle owner: name, phone number, address or meeting place, vehicle details, and, where a fitment requires it, the owner's identity number or a scan of the identity-document barcode and their signature. This information is used only to complete and verify the job. It is stored on our servers; the App keeps a working copy on your phone only so jobs can be done without signal, and clears it when you sign out, except changes that have not been sent yet, which wait on the phone until they can be sent.

4. How we use the information

  • Assign, dispatch and track jobs, and confirm that work was done where and when it was recorded;
  • Keep technicians in contact with the office and each other, including in emergencies (the radio and SOS features);
  • Pay, support and manage technicians;
  • Find and fix faults and improve the App;
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use it to track anyone across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Who we share it with

Only service providers that run parts of the App for us, under contract and only for these purposes: our cloud hosting and database providers, LiveKit (voice), Google Firebase and Expo (push notifications and app updates), PostHog (analytics) and Sentry (crash reporting). Some of these providers process data outside South Africa, with safeguards that POPIA requires. We disclose information to authorities only where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

Job records, photos, signatures and location history linked to a job are kept for as long as we need them to support the customer's tracking contract and to meet legal and accounting requirements. Session recordings are deleted after 30 days. When you stop working with us your App account is disabled; you can ask us to delete personal information we no longer need.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can turn off location, camera, microphone or notification access in your phone's settings at any time, but the App needs them to do its job and will ask for them again.
  • Under POPIA you may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it, by emailing ceo@sharedmobility.africa. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

8. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, your sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage, and access to our systems is limited to staff who need it.

9. Children

The App is for adult technicians only and is not directed at children.

10. Changes

We will update this page when the App's handling of personal information changes, and change the date above.
